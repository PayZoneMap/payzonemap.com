What PayZoneMap is

PayZoneMap shows the US pay zones that companies publish on their own careers pages and job postings, side by side, matched to places. It's a reference to help you understand where you might land and what to ask a recruiter or manager. It isn't employment, legal, tax or financial advice, and it isn't an offer or a promise of pay from anyone.

Where the information comes from

  • Each company's zones come from that company's own public page, linked in its row with the date we last checked it. Where a company lists cities or regions, we match them to metro areas and counties, and each row says what we had to interpret.
  • Companies change their zones, and a recruiter can place a specific address differently. The company's own answer for your location is the one that counts.
  • We keep a dated copy of each company's source whenever its zones change, a record of every check, and a copy at the Internet Archive where it accepts one, so we can show what a company published and when.
  • Posted pay ranges come from companies' current public job postings. Each range is shown as the company posted it, for one zone, with what it covers in the company's own words and a link to the posting. The level beside it is our match from the job title, not the company's level. Ranges that cover different things are never combined, and postings change or close, so the posting itself is the current word.
  • Price figures are the US Bureau of Economic Analysis Regional Price Parities. The cost bands are our own rough grouping of that index.

Corrections

If a zone is out of date, or you work at one of these companies and want something corrected or removed, email hello@payzonemap.com. Tell us the company and, if you can, a link to the current page. We check every request against the company's own published information.

Each company's row has a "Was this wrong?" link for telling us the zone you were actually given. It opens an email to us with the place and company filled in. A person reads every report, and a zone only changes when the company's own information supports it. Reports we accept appear beside the published zone as "Reported by people", counted by zone and month. We keep only the company, place, zone, who told you (recruiter, offer or posting), whether the job is remote, hybrid or in the office, and the month. Never your name, email address or pay.

Privacy

  • There are no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no analytics.
  • The place you type or tap stays in your browser. It's added to the page address (for example #c06033) so you can bookmark or share it, and the site never receives it.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts load from Google Fonts and the map code loads from jsDelivr. Like any website, those services receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads, under their own privacy policies.
  • If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply and to make the correction.

Using what you find here

You're welcome to share links and screenshots, and to bring what you find to a recruiter, a manager or a pay conversation. If you republish the zone data, please credit PayZoneMap.com and link to the company's own page.

PayZoneMap is provided as is. We work to keep it accurate and current, but we can't guarantee that every zone is complete or up to date, and we aren't responsible for decisions made using it. Always confirm your zone with the company.
